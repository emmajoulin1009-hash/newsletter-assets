01 Markets This Week · 15 Jun 2026

The Headlines That Moved the Tape

US Strikes Iran a Second Day; Oil Jumps, Stocks Slide

US strikes on Iran ran a second day on 10 June; the S&P fell −1.6% and the Nasdaq −2% as Iran retaliated against US facilities in Bahrain, Jordan and Kuwait. Brent jumped above ~$96 with the Strait of Hormuz near-closed, and US crude stocks fell 7.2M bbl, a seventh straight draw. Risk-off flows favored defensives.

 

Portfolio impact: An unhedged energy or transport tilt swings with every Hormuz headline; the FOMC meets 16–17 June, so a rate-sensitive book sits in the path.

CPI Runs Hot at 4.2%, but Core Cools to 2.9%

May CPI rose +0.5% MoM / +4.2% YoY, the hottest since April 2023, with energy roughly 60% of the rise. Core cooled to +0.2% MoM / +2.9% YoY, below the 0.3% estimate. Higher-for-longer stays intact into the 16–17 June FOMC.

 

Portfolio impact: A soft core keeps a cut on the table, but a hot headline anchors long rates; a duration-heavy book stays exposed until the Fed signals.

A Defensive-Quality Bid, Not a Broad Rebound

Only 9 of 39 markets closed positive. US Managed Care led at +6.26% WTD and Hong Kong Aerospace & Defense ran +8.0%, while an Asia-semis bounce (Taiwan Passive Components +5.0%) was short-covering, not a confirmed turn.

 

Portfolio impact: A static hold of the crowded AI book fell −12% over the window: an AI or mega-cap-heavy portfolio is concentrated in the factor that just repriced.
