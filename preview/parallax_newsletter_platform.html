01 Markets This Week  ·  Rolling 7 days to 9 Jun 2026

Three moves that reshaped the factor map.

Payrolls shock snaps the nine-week streak; the curve reprices

May NFP printed +172K vs an 80K consensus, with prior months revised up; CME hike-by-year-end odds jumped near 72%, the 10Y above 4.5% and the 30Y above 5%. The S&P fell −2.6% on the week, its first weekly loss since March; the Nasdaq shed −4.18% Friday, its worst session since April 2025.

 

Portfolio impact: Duration sleeves built for 2026 cuts took mark-to-market damage; chips whipsawed, Micron +9% and Nvidia +2% Monday 8 June (S&P +0.30% to 7,405.73) before a Tuesday fade (S&P −0.26% to 7,386.65) on Iran-strike headlines. CPI 10 June, FOMC 16–17 June.

Israel-Iran escalation and a fragile ceasefire whipsaw oil

Brent crossed ~$98 then fell toward $88–94 as Iran said it ended operations and a US-brokered ceasefire held intermittently; Hormuz traffic stayed well below pre-war levels and OPEC+ added 188K bpd for July.

 

Portfolio impact: The oil round-trip reopens the inflation question the curve had started to close; Tuesday wobbled on renewed-strike headlines, leaving Tactical −0.9% WTD as energy and tech led the red sectors.

Parallax telemetry: a defensive-quality bid, not a broad rebound

Only 9 of 39 markets closed positive; US Managed Care +6.26% WTD led the US tape and Hong Kong Aerospace & Defense +8.0%, while momentum long/short broke −4.2% in France and −2.2% in Germany.

 

Portfolio impact: Money fled crowded growth into defensive and quality names; Asia semis (Taiwan Passive Components +5.0% WTD, Korea Semi Equipment) bounced on short-covering, without the breadth to confirm a trend repair.
