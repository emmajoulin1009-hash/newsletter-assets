01   This Week

Five Moves Reshaping Institutional Positioning.

Rolling window to 9 June 2026 · sourced from BLS, CME FedWatch, CNBC, Reuters, Parallax

• Payrolls Shock Snaps the Nine-Week Streak as the Curve Reprices

NFP +172K (80K est.) pushed CME hike odds near 72%, the 10Y above 4.5% and the 30Y above 5%; S&P −2.6% WTD, its first weekly loss since March.

• Chips Whipsaw: Micron +9%, Nvidia +2% Monday, Then a Tuesday Fade

After Friday's 4.2% Nasdaq rout, Monday rebounded (S&P +0.30%, Nasdaq +0.86%) as Jensen Huang called the drop a buy; Tuesday reversed on renewed Iran-strike headlines.

• Israel-Iran Escalation and a Fragile Ceasefire Whipsaw Oil

Brent crossed ~$98 then fell toward $88–94 on a fragile US-brokered Israel-Iran ceasefire; Hormuz traffic stayed below pre-war levels and OPEC+ added 188K bpd for July.

• CPI Lands Wednesday Into a Hawkish Setup; FOMC 16–17 June Next

May CPI (due 10 June) was forecast near +4.2% YoY headline, +2.9% core; PPI Thursday. Futures price a near-certain June hold but a ~30% December hike.

• Parallax Telemetry: a Defensive-Quality Bid, Not a Broad Rebound

Only 9 of 39 markets positive WTD; US Managed Care +6.26% led and Aerospace & Defense +8.0%, while Asia semis bounced on short-covering as Momentum fell −2.6%.
