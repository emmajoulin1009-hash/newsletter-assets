01 Markets This Week · 29 Jun 2026

The Headlines That Moved the Tape

The Fed Holds, but Warsh Flips the Dot Plot to a Hike

The FOMC held at 3.50–3.75% at Warsh's first meeting but the dot plot flipped to a hike, and September-hike odds jumped to roughly 68% from 29% a week earlier. The S&P 500 closed the week at 7,358 and the Nasdaq near 25,477.

 

Portfolio impact
A duration-heavy book sits in the path of the new dot plot; the 2-year had its biggest Fed-day move since 2008, repricing any rate-sensitive tilt.

A $1.3 Trillion AI-Chip Unwind Sweeps the Tape

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index ran more than 6% to a record into Thursday and the Nasdaq rallied roughly 2% on the 17th as chips powered a comeback from the Fed selloff. Intel jumped about 9% after President Trump said Apple agreed to design and build chips with Intel in the US.

 

Portfolio impact
The unwind hit the most-crowded factor; Parallax shows US Clinical Biotech +9.2% WTD leading as US breadth held at 30 of 43 baskets.

Oil Slides to a Pre-War Low as the Strait of Hormuz Reopens

Brent fell to roughly $73.50, down 4.6% and its lowest since before the US–Iran war, as Hormuz tanker traffic resumed and peace talks progressed; the IEA put UAE exports near 85% of pre-war levels.

 

Portfolio impact
Lower oil eases the inflation tail but pressured energy baskets across the week.
