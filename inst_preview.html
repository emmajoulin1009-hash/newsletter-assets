01   This Week

Five Moves Reshaping Institutional Positioning.

Rolling window to 10 June 2026 · sourced from BLS, CME FedWatch, CNBC, Reuters, Parallax

• US Strikes Iran a Second Day; Stocks Sell Off

S&P −1.6%, Nasdaq −2% on 10 Jun; Iran retaliated against US facilities in Bahrain, Jordan and Kuwait. Risk-off flows favored defensives.

• Oil Jumps as the Strait of Hormuz Stays Near-Closed

Brent climbed above $96; near-total Hormuz closure persists; US crude inventories fell 7.2M bbl, a 7th straight weekly draw.

• CPI Runs Hot at 4.2% Headline, but Core Cools to 2.9%

May CPI +0.5% MoM / +4.2% YoY, hottest since April 2023 (energy ~60% of the rise); core +0.2% MoM / +2.9% YoY, below the 0.3% estimate. Higher-for-longer intact; FOMC 16–17 Jun ahead.

• Payrolls Shock Still Sets the Tape; Momentum Unwinds

May NFP +172K vs 80K consensus pushed hike odds near 72% and the 10Y above 4.5%; the crowded AI/momentum trade kept bleeding (a static hold of the book fell −12% over the window).

• Parallax Telemetry: a Defensive-Quality Bid, Not a Broad Rebound

Only 9 of 39 markets positive WTD; US Managed Care +6.26% WTD leads, Hong Kong Aerospace & Defense +8.0%; Asia semis (Taiwan Passive Components +5.0%) bounce on short-covering without cross-market confirmation.
