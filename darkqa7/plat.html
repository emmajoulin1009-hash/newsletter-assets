01 Markets This Week  ·  Rolling 7 days to 24 Jun 2026

Three moves that reshaped the factor map.

The Fed Holds, but Warsh Flips the Dot Plot to a Hike

The FOMC held at 3.50–3.75% but stripped the easing bias; the dot-plot median rose to 3.8% and futures moved the next move toward an October hike.

 

Portfolio impact: The hawkish turn pressures rate-sensitive property and long-duration growth; the 2-year had its biggest Fed-day move since 2008. Stress your book against a no-cut path.

A $1.3 Trillion AI-Chip Unwind Sweeps the Tape

The Nasdaq fell 2.21% Tuesday as roughly $1.3T in AI-chip value unwound on a Broadcom-led contagion. Parallax flagged the mega-cap AI fatigue on the Fed day, before the worst of the drop.

 

Portfolio impact: The unwind hit the most-crowded factor; US breadth held at 30 of 43 baskets as defensives bid. Check where your AI exposure actually sits.

Oil Slides to a Pre-War Low as the Strait of Hormuz Reopens

Brent fell to roughly $73.50, down 4.6% and its lowest since before the US–Iran war, as Hormuz tanker traffic resumed. Lower oil eases the inflation tail but pressures energy baskets.

 

Portfolio impact: The Hormuz risk premium is draining out fast; energy and oilfield services led the downside, easing the inflation overhang even as the Fed turned hawkish.
