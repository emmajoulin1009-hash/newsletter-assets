01 Markets This Week · 15 Jun 2026

The Headlines That Moved the Tape

The Fed Decides Today; Warsh Chairs His First Meeting

The FOMC concludes today with a hold widely expected, target 3.50–3.75% (CME near 97% no-change). Markets watch the dot plot and Warsh's first press conference for the path into year-end; inflation at a three-year high has shifted the bias from easing toward neutral.

 

Portfolio impact: A duration-heavy book sits in the path of the dot plot; a hawkish hold reprices long rates and any rate-sensitive tilt.

Chips Round-Trip a Trillion-Dollar Selloff, Then Lead the Rebound

A roughly $1.4T AI-semis drawdown (SOX −10% on 6 Jun, Broadcom −12.6%) reversed sharply this week: Micron +9.9%, Intel +11.2% early-week as buyers treated the dip as technical. Parallax flags US semi-equipment fatigue (YTD +82.4%) even as Asia semis lead.

 

Portfolio impact: Parallax flags US semi-equipment fatigue at +82.4% YTD even as Asia semis lead the tape.

Oil Slides Toward $80 as US–Iran De-escalation Reopens Hormuz

Brent fell roughly 3% Tuesday and 4.8% Monday to near $80, a fourth straight down session and the lowest since early March, after a US–Iran agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz; OPEC+ quotas and UAE output add supply. US Oilfield Services −5.4% WTD was the weakest global basket.

 

Portfolio impact: US Oilfield Services −5.4% WTD was the weakest global basket on the week.
