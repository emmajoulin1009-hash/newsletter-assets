02   Client Questions

Three Questions Your Clients Will Ask You This Week.

29 Jun 2026

"Should I be worried about my AI and tech concentration?"

The risk is concentration in the one trade the Fed just repriced. The AI complex shed roughly $1.3T on the week and Korea tripped circuit breakers, while US breadth held at 30 of 43 baskets. A cap-weighted index quietly concentrates you in exactly that repriced leadership.

Evidence to cite: ~$1.3T AI-chip value unwound; KR aggregate −8.0% WTD with circuit breakers tripped twice (CNBC / Parallax).

"Where did capital actually go when AI cracked?"

Into defensive quality. As mega-cap AI unwound, US Clinical Biotech rose +9.2% WTD and Homebuilding +5.7%, while insurers and staples bid. A book that owns defensive quality, not just the AI leg, captured the rotation the index missed.

Evidence to cite: US Clinical Biotech +9.2% WTD, US Homebuilding +5.7% daily; defensive-quality basket +4.18% vs ACWI −2.41% (Parallax).

"What does the hawkish Fed mean for my bonds?"

The 2-year just had its biggest Fed-day jump since 2008 as the dot plot flipped to a hike; Bank of America now sees three quarter-point hikes this year. Until that resolves, duration stays under pressure and rate-sensitive property remains the funding source.

Evidence to cite: 2-year +16bp near 4.18%, 10-year ~4.45%, BofA sees three hikes to 4.25–4.5% (CNBC / Fortune).

04   What Other Advisors Aren't Saying Yet

Three Contrarian Reads Worth Raising With Clients.

“The $1.3T AI unwind is a factor event, not a tech dip.”

The drop looks like a tech wobble, but it was the crowded AI factor unwinding into a hawkish Fed, with Korea tripping circuit breakers. Most advisors will call it a buying dip; few will flag that a cap-weighted index just concentrated clients in the repriced factor.

“Defensive quality, not bonds, was the ballast that worked this week.”

As AI cracked, insurers, homebuilders, staples and utilities bid while the 2-year spiked. The uncomfortable truth few raise: a 60/40 leaning only on bonds for ballast missed the defensive-equity rotation that actually moved.

“The bond 'safe haven' just took its biggest Fed-day hit since 2008.”

The 2-year's 16bp Fed-day spike is the kind of move most clients assume only equities make, and BofA now sees three hikes this year. Advisors who framed the long bond as ballast owe clients an honest read on duration risk if the next move is a hike, not a cut.
