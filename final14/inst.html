01   This Week

Five Moves Reshaping Institutional Positioning.

Rolling window to 24 June 2026 · sourced from CNBC, Bloomberg, CNN, Fortune, Parallax

• The Fed Holds, but Warsh Flips the Dot Plot to a Hike

The FOMC held at 3.50–3.75% but the dot plot flipped to a hike: nine of eighteen officials now pencil one before year-end, and September-hike odds jumped to roughly 68% from 29% a week earlier.

• A $1.3 Trillion AI-Chip Unwind Sweeps the Tape

The Nasdaq fell 2.21% Tuesday as roughly $1.3 trillion in AI-chip value unwound, Broadcom's post-earnings fade igniting the contagion. Parallax flagged the mega-cap AI and semis fatigue on the Fed day, before the worst of the drop.

• The 2-Year Yield Spikes the Most Since 2008 on the Fed Day

The 2-year Treasury yield jumped more than 16bp on the Fed day, its biggest move since March 2008, and held near 4.18% while the 10-year hovered 4.45%. Bank of America now sees three quarter-point hikes to 4.25–4.5%.

• Korea Trips Circuit Breakers as the AI Selloff Hits Asia

South Korea's Kospi fell roughly 10% intraday, tripping circuit breakers twice, as SK Hynix and Samsung each shed about 12%; Japan's Nikkei fell 3.6%. Parallax: KR aggregate −8.0% WTD, the week's weakest major market.

• Oil Slides to a Pre-War Low as the Strait of Hormuz Reopens

Brent fell to roughly $73.50 Wednesday, down 4.6% and its lowest since before the US–Iran war, as tanker traffic resumed through the Strait of Hormuz. Lower oil eases the inflation tail but pressures energy baskets.

Based on Live Parallax Telemetry Across 62K Securities.

The Hawkish Turn Detonated the AI Trade

Data: The dot plot flipped to a hike, the 2-year spiked 16bp, and a $1.3T AI unwind followed, yet the rotation book returned +2.39% over 15 to 24 Jun vs ACWI −2.41%.

Implication: The risk was concentrated in the crowded AI leg the Fed just repriced; run a factor-drift check against a higher-for-longer, no-cut path.

US Defensives Did the Heavy Lifting

Data: US Clinical Biotech (+9.2% WTD) and Homebuilding (+5.7% daily) led while South Korea's Auto and Construction cyclicals fell −13.5% WTD and the Kospi tripped circuit breakers.

Implication: That is money rotating to defensive quality, not broad risk appetite; a book leaning on the AI leg for returns is carrying the most-repriced factor.

Asia and Energy Funded the Move

Data: Korea fell −8.0% WTD and Hong Kong property developers −4.7% WTD, while Brent slid 4.6% to $73.50 as the Strait of Hormuz reopened.

Implication: Cap-weighted global indices carry the Asia AI-supply-chain drawdown; check both the regional and the energy exposures hiding in your book.
